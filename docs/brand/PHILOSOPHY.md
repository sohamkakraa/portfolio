# Philosophy

> **The cosmos teaches those who wander.**

The first page of the brand book. Everything on sohamkakra.com is checked against this page before it goes live. If something does not fit, it does not ship, however much we like it.

Status: **draft**. Sections marked ✍️ are waiting for Soham's own words.

---

## Who this is

A living being on planet Earth, curious about the universe, the earth and himself. A traveller, not a tourist. Kind before clever. Laughs a lot. Stops on every hike to take the photo, however heavy the bag.

**Soham** (सोऽहम्) is read here as *oneness with everything*. Not "I", but "all of this, together".

## Who it is for

Fellow travellers, friends, and anyone who wants to learn alongside. After a visit, someone should be able to write, read a trip, feel a little inspired, or join the next adventure.

---

## The master angle: three contrasts

Every page, chapter and interaction should show at least one of these tensions. Together they are the "eye" the whole site looks through.

| Contrast | Draft sentence | Soham's words ✍️ |
|---|---|---|
| **The vast and the small** | The universe is enormous, and it reveals itself in small, slow moments. | |
| **Still and wandering** | You learn the world by moving through it, and understand it by sitting still. | |
| **Science and soul** | Everything can be explained, and still be full of wonder. | |

**What a visitor should feel:** curious, and genuinely interested in what the site is about. ✍️

---

## The absolutes

Things that are always true, on every page. ✍️ *Soham to edit, add or remove.*

1. **Vast and small on every screen.** Something cosmic and something tiny and human.
2. **Every page teaches one thing.**
3. **One loud thing per screen.** Everything else is quiet night and soft light.
4. **Made, not borrowed.** Our own photographs and our own physics-based illustrations. No stock imagery.
5. **Every story ends with an open door.** Write to Soham, or join the next trip.

## Things we never do

- Clutter (the Amazon problem), bold-for-the-sake-of-bold, or loud-luxury styling.
- Autoplay anything without a clear choice. Track anyone without consent.
- Publish machine-written content without Soham reading and approving it.
- Draw borders around people. Countries are names of places, not walls.

## The process: The Wanderer's Method

Adapted from a six-step videography process presented by a creative director: capture, connect, angle, structure, improvise, refine.

| # | Step | What we do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Gather light** | Capture everything in Notion: learnings, photos, songs, quotes, half-ideas. Messy is fine. |
| 2 | **Find the web** | Connect things that seem unrelated. Surprise comes from contrast. |
| 3 | **Choose the eye** | One sentence and one feeling, ideally a contrast. *What is this really about? What should people feel?* |
| 4 | **Set the orbit** | Write the absolutes and a skeleton: the minimum that must exist. |
| 5 | **Wander** | Improvise inside the orbit. New ideas are tried in `/lab` before they touch real pages. |
| 6 | **Bring into focus** | Keep only what fits the angle. Cut the rest, even favourites. Make it personal. |

It runs at two levels:

- **The whole site** uses the master angle above. Changed rarely, on purpose.
- **Each chapter** (a trip, a project, a season) gets its own card: see [`CHAPTER_TEMPLATE.md`](./CHAPTER_TEMPLATE.md).

### On automated proposals

Generated text pulls everything toward the average. Automation may **gather and propose** (steps 1, 2 and 5), never **choose the angle or refine** (steps 3 and 6). Every proposal is reviewed live by Soham before it is published.

### Words we avoid

- **"AI"** in site copy. It is a fashion word and says nothing about the actual work. Describe what was built, for whom, and what it changed.

## The story: a star's life

The site follows one star from birth to the cosmic web. The star is Soham's journey.

| Stage | What it is | On the site |
|---|---|---|
| Nursery | Pillars of gas and dust, like the Pillars of Creation | The opening: tagline and intro |
| Ignition | A push (a nearby supernova's shockwave, or two clouds colliding) squeezes one pocket past its tipping point (the Jeans mass). It collapses, heats up, and fusion starts at ~10 million K | Where it began, and what pushed it |
| Main sequence | A hot blue-white star with planets, moons and meteors | Memories: places are planets, photos are moons, small moments are meteors |
| Red supergiant | The star swells and cools; inner orbits are swallowed | Now: current work and what is growing |
| Supernova | Each journey ends in light and scatters what it made | Finished chapters |
| Cosmic web | Endings become knots in the web | The archive, oneness with everything, and the open door |

Comets cross the screen between stages, each carrying a learning back as a refresher.

## The cosmic map

| Content | Object | Why |

| A learning | Comet | Comets return on an orbit, like a refresher. |
| An open question | Black hole | Nothing escapes, yet it shapes everything around it. |
| Connections between things | Cosmic web | The real filaments of gas and dark matter linking galaxies. |
| Influences (films, quotes, people) | Gravitational lensing | Something massive bends how everything behind it looks. |

## Perspective: the three eyes

| Eye | Telescope | Shows |
|---|---|---|
| Visible | Hubble | The story and the photos |
| Infrared | Webb, sees through dust | What was learned and how it felt |
| X-ray | Chandra, sees the hottest places | The facts: places, dates, camera settings, coordinates |

## Voice

Warm, curious, a little funny, never preachy. English throughout. Hindi only for quotes, words and phrases that are Hindi, always with an English translation beside them. Places are named plainly ("Hampi, India"); coordinates live in the X-ray layer.
